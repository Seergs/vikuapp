This dummy article lets you check numbering, spacing, and text wrapping in Help.

2. Choose a project.

7. Check that the numbers line up consistently.
8. Check the list in light and dark appearance.
9. Check the list with a larger text size.
10. This two-digit item helps check the space between the number and its text.

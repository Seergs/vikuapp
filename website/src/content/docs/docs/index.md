Viku is designed to be simple and intuitive. These guides provide extra help with
setup, features, and anything that needs a little more explanation.

If something in the app feels confusing or you needed a guide to complete an
everyday task, please [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/Seergs/vikuapp/issues).
Tell us what you were trying to do and where you got stuck. Your feedback helps us
make Viku clearer and easier to use, so you can spend less time reading docs and
more time getting things done.
